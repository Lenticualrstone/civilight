- WLD-60 detailed-terrain `강 · 길` drawing by the author uses `MapEdits.updateMap`; no dedicated helper flags a hand-drawn line beyond `DrawnLine.origin/editedByAuthor`.

## Defaults used

- Term rules: `TermAccess(hiddenUnlocked)` gates candidates of hidden terms; `맞음` excluded from all `▲` counts and tagged `맞음` on the card (Q-07); terms without reveal go to `아직 공개 전` with `첫 공개 없음` (Q-08); a reveal on a whole choice block is allowed, an option line is refused with `선택지 보기 줄에는 첫 공개를 걸 수 없음` (Q-06); direct `전환 문구` is game-output text (Q-05); matcher options of Q-04 (a)–(h) as proposed behind `MatcherOptions`; more than 10 `아님` shows `글자 찾기 끄기`.
- A reveal anchored on a block that is not live reads as `근거 지워짐` even before the anchor engine relocates the record (read-time resolution of the model).
- Time: `연도만` years have 365 days and the story starts on day 1 of the start year (Q-13); flashback sub labels `<사건> 그날 · 전 · 뒤`; timeline row ages = characters tagged `주연` (Q-14); story rows = authored story events plus chapter spans (Q-15).
- 이곳의 시간: events at the place or at a map region holding one of its pins; at equal time a world event precedes a scene; rows after the lens go below the divider; flashbacks at their own era (Q-24).
- 인물 동선: scenes without 때 sort at the time of the previous timed scene in manuscript order; position at a lens = last stop (time order) among stops whose scene starts at or before the lens.
- Maps: `리` = 0.4 km, `직접` unit has no km (Q-19); one pin per place per map; pick radius 0.02; region kinds free text (Q-17); route `쓰인 곳` not shown (Q-18).
- Generator `gen-1`: 64 × 48 grid; land fractions 0.35 / 0.5 / 0.68; ridges as gaussian bumps along seeded polylines; rivers on an 8-neighbour priority flood with seeded meander; climate from moisture quantiles (dry .18, wet .62, swamp .95 and low), no temperature model; towns from coast and lowland only (not rivers), so re-rolling `강` changes only the river layer; roads = minimum spanning tree per landmass, straight edges; polygon = 96 Lloyd-relaxed sites, 8 provinces with empty names (no generated names); hex 32 × 24 pointy 24 km with a matching scale. Re-rolling an upstream step recomputes the steps below it with their own seeds (Q-22); a stored map from another generator version refuses re-roll. Result JSON keys: `version, grid, land, coastline, heights, relief, ridges, rivers, lakes, climate, towns, roads`.
- Text conditions: kinds `항구 도시 · 산맥 · 사막 · 숲 · 늪 · 설원 · 호수 · 강 · 바다 · 도시 · 평원` (and synonyms); an unknown 무엇 is kept and marked `확인 필요`; a clause without direction covers the whole map; `해안` with another kind is a trait shown as `남쪽 해안`.
